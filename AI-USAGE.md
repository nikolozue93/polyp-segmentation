# AI Usage Disclosure

This project was developed with assistance from OpenAI's ChatGPT.

AI assistance was used for:

- Explaining PyTorch training, validation, segmentation metrics, and optimization concepts.
- Drafting portions of the training, evaluation, loss-function, and inference-benchmarking implementations.
- Helping debug implementation and environment issues.
- Providing guidance on experimental design and documentation.

The inference benchmarking implementation was primarily AI-generated. I executed it in Google Colab, measured FP32 and mixed-precision performance on the selected trained model, and recorded the results.

I was responsible for running the experiments, inspecting their outputs, comparing model configurations, interpreting segmentation failures, and making final project decisions.

The reported accuracy and performance results come from actual executions, not AI-generated estimates.

AI-generated components are identified here for transparency. Their inclusion does not imply that the underlying algorithms are original.
